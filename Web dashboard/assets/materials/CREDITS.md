# Surface material credits

Photographic diffuse and OpenGL normal maps (1K), downloaded from the official Poly Haven asset server and served locally.

- [Aerial Grass Rock](https://polyhaven.com/a/aerial_grass_rock)
- [Castle Brick 01](https://polyhaven.com/a/castle_brick_01)
- [Roof Slates 02](https://polyhaven.com/a/roof_slates_02)

All assets are CC0-1.0: [Poly Haven asset license](https://polyhaven.com/license). Exact source URLs and local filenames are retained in `sources.json`.

The same assets also have locally bundled 4K diffuse and normal-map sources for close-up views. See `highResolutionAssets` in `sources.json`; source authors and CC0 licensing are unchanged.
