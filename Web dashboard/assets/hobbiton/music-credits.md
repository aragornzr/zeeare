# By the Garden Gate

Original instrumental composition and deterministic instrument synthesis created for this atlas, October 2026. No movie soundtrack, recorded performance, voice imitation, or third-party audio samples are used. This is an original pastoral folk cue, not music from the films.

40 seconds, stereo 44.1 kHz, 16-bit PCM WAV. Lilting 6/8 at 72 dotted-quarter beats per minute. Instruments: damped plucked-string synthesis, breathy harmonic flute, soft bowed bass and fifths, stereo room reflections. The last phrase resolves into a quiet tail, with fades at both ends. Source: `tools/render-hobbiton-music.py`; fixed random seed makes the rendering repeatable. The synthesizer and composition are project assets under the same terms as the application.

Playback requires the viewer's sound control. Pause the transport when the document is hidden or a video player takes over. Use `music.capture.stream.getAudioTracks()` when recording a live tour. The WAV also supplies the embedded audio in the exported tour.
