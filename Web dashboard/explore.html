A fan-made atlas focused on the Third Age. The world backdrop and illustrated destinations are artistic interpretations. The Rivendell and Lothlórien views use artwork supplied by the user; their labels and scenery are illustrative, not surveyed geographic data. All 118 entries have cinematic tours and interactive 3D views using detailed procedural terrain, architecture, photographic materials and image-based foliage. Moria uses detailed image-based characters in a 3D hall. Supplied illustrated maps remain available through the Illustrated view control. Local layouts and schematic routes are artistic interpretations. Earlier-age and uncertain locations are included in the index without invented map positions. This is a broad collection of named places, not an exhaustive catalogue of every name across Tolkien’s writings.
